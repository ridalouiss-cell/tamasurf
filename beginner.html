Beginner Surf Lesson

Your first steps into surfing

You want to learn how to surf and you have little or no experience? My Beginner Surf Course is the perfect way to start your surfing journey in a relaxed and supportive environment.

You’ll learn the basics of surfing, including warm-up exercises, paddling technique, the take-off and riding your first waves. Safety in the water, understanding waves and currents and respecting the ocean are always a top priority.

Lessons take place private or in small groups to provide personal coaching and fast progress. No matter your age or fitness level, everyone is welcome. Most importantly, we focus on having fun and building confidence in the water.

Jump in, feel the ocean’s energy and catch your first waves!
