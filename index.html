Welcome to Tamasurf Morocco

About me

I grew up in the small village of Tamawanza, right by the ocean, and started surfing at the age of 10. Surfing is more than a sport to me. It's my lifestyle. As a surf coach, I'm passionate about sharing my experience and love for the ocean with others.

My vision

From surfing and surfskating to swimming, diving and fishing, the ocean is where I feel at home. I love helping every surfer, beginner to advanced, feel confident, safe and stoked in the water.

Process

Your Surf Day, Step by Step

Learning to surf made easy, safe and fun. No experience needed.

01

Book

Send me a message on WhatsApp and tell me your dates and level, and I'll help you pick the session that fits you best.

02

Meet & Warm Up

We meet on the sand, loosen up together and go through a quick safety briefing so you feel ready before we paddle out.

03

Lesson

Two hours in the water with me, one-on-one or in a small group, with feedback tailored to exactly where you are.

04

Lunch at the Beach

On full-day packages we share a fresh lunch on the beach. Time to rest, refuel and swap stories before the next surf.

05

Free Surf

Two more hours to put it all into practice and just enjoy the waves, with me close by whenever you need a hand.

06

Progress

We look back on how your session went and map out your next step, from whitewater to green waves to point breaks.
