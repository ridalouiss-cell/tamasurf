Intermediate Lesson

The Intermediate Surf Course is designed for surfers who already catch green waves by themselves and want to improve their technique, confidence and overall performance in a variety of conditions.

We focus on advanced wave reading, positioning, timing and stronger paddling, helping you catch better waves and stay in control. You’ll work on cleaner take-offs, smoother turns, speed generation and linking maneuvers, allowing you to surf with more flow and style.

Take your surfing to the next level and unlock your full potential.
